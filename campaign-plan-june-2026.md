# Safety Margin: 1-Month Lead Generation Campaign

**Campaign period:** June 10 – July 9, 2026
**Primary channel:** Facebook / Meta (paid + organic)
**Budget:** ~₱1,000/month paid spend

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Campaign name:** "Know Your Gap"

One sentence: Drive Filipino adults to take the Safety Margin quiz, discover their financial protection gap, and book a consultation with Jojo.

**Primary objective:** Generate 60 qualified funnel leads (full report unlocks) within 30 days from Facebook.

**Secondary objectives:**
- Grow Safety Margin Facebook page followers by 10–15% organically
- Build brand recognition across all 6 audience segments
- Seed the Messenger pipeline with warm leads for follow-up

---

## 2. Target audience

Safety Margin serves six segments. For a 1-month campaign with ₱1,000 in paid spend, you can only target one or two at paid scale. The remaining segments are covered organically.

**Paid (primary):** Young professionals (pro) and parents/families. These two are the most active on Facebook, most likely to engage quiz-style content, and have the clearest emotional hook (starting out vs. protecting the family).

**Organic (all six segments):** Rotate one post per week per segment theme to ensure full coverage and grow reach.

Pain points by segment:

| Segment | Core fear | Emotional hook |
|---|---|---|
| Pro | "I don't know if I'm covered." | First job, first paycheck, first real risk |
| Family | "What happens to them if something happens to me?" | Provider guilt |
| OFW | "I sacrifice so much — is it protected?" | Sacrifice and distance |
| Entrepreneur | "My business and I are the same person." | No sick days, no backup |
| Business | "My team depends on me staying healthy." | Obligation at scale |
| HNW | "I built it. I need to preserve it." | Legacy and estate continuity |

---

## 3. Key messages

**Core campaign message:** Most Filipinos are underprotected and don't know it. Take the 3-minute quiz to find out where your gaps are.

**Supporting messages:**

1. "Your score shows you exactly what's missing." (tangible, specific output)
2. "It's free, takes 3 minutes, and no sales pitch is attached to the quiz." (removes friction and suspicion)
3. "Jojo reviews every report personally." (human + credibility)
4. "Protection gaps don't wait for the right time." (urgency without fearmongering)

**Tone:** Warm, direct, Filipino-friendly. Short sentences. No jargon. Taglish is fine for younger/pro segment posts. Serious but not scary.

**Copy rules:**
- No em dashes
- No insurance company names in public-facing copy
- CTA always goes to safetymargin.app/funnel or the segment-specific page

---

## 4. Channel strategy

### Facebook paid ads (₱1,000 budget)

Run one ad set with two ad variations. Target Metro Manila + nearby provinces, ages 24–45, interests: personal finance, OFW communities, parenting, small business.

- **Ad A:** Curiosity angle. Headline: "Kumusta ang coverage mo?" Short copy, quiz CTA.
- **Ad B:** Social proof angle. Headline: "Most Filipinos are missing at least 2 protection gaps." Copy leads with the stat, CTA to find out theirs.

Budget split: ₱700 to the better-performing ad after day 7, ₱300 to the other.

Expected reach at ₱1,000: approximately 8,000–15,000 impressions, 150–400 link clicks.

### Facebook organic (Safety Margin page)

**Cadence:** 3–4 posts per week. Rotate across content types.

**Content types:**
- Segment spotlight posts (one per segment per week, rotate)
- "Did you know?" financial protection fact posts
- Jojo's personal insight / behind-the-scenes posts
- Quiz CTA posts (1–2 per week max)

### Messenger (follow-up channel)

Set a saved reply template for new leads: acknowledge the report, ask one open question, offer the Calendly link.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

**Risk 1: Low quiz completion rate.**
Mitigation: Check funnel drop-off data in Supabase after week 1. If preview-to-capture conversion is below 30%, tweak the capture page copy.

**Risk 2: Ad fatigue on a small budget.**
Mitigation: Refresh ad copy (not the image) at the 2-week mark.

**Risk 3: Organic posts don't reach enough new people.**
Mitigation: Post at peak times (7–9 PM PHT). Ask 2–3 friends or clients to share the launch post in week 1 to seed engagement.
