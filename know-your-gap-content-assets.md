### EDUCATION / FACT POSTS (Nice-to-have)

---

#### Fact Post 1: The coverage amount problem
*Shareable, educational*

Here's something most Filipinos don't realize.

A lot of people have insurance. Fewer have enough coverage.

A policy that seemed sufficient 10 years ago might not cover what you need today. Medical costs have risen. Lifestyle expenses have changed. And most people haven't revisited their coverage since they first signed up.

Having protection and having the right protection are two different things.

Do you know what yours actually covers? Take 3 minutes to find out: safetymargin.app/funnel

---

#### Fact Post 2: The income replacement gap
*Week 1 or 2, educational*

If you couldn't work for 6 months, how long would your family's finances hold?

Most financial planners recommend 6 to 12 months of income replacement coverage as a baseline. Most Filipino households have significantly less than that.

3 minutes to find out where you stand: safetymargin.app/funnel

---

#### Fact Post 3: Why "later" costs more
*Week 2 or 3, educational*

A lot of young professionals say the same thing: "I'll think about protection when I'm older."

Here's what that actually costs.

Protection premiums are based on your age and health at the time you apply. The younger and healthier you are, the lower your rate, for the life of the policy.

Waiting until you need it almost always means paying more for less.

Take 3 minutes and find out where you stand today: safetymargin.app/funnel
